# Recording notes — 09 Banking

## Who speaks first
**Bank agent (English)** with the unauthorized charges.

## Pace & pauses
- Amount: “three hundred nineteen dollars” (not “319” mumbled).
- After lock/dispute explanation: pause for customer confirmation.
- “five to seven business days” spoken cleanly.

## Overlaps
None. Customer affirmations stay short.

## When the demo should shine
1. **didn't authorize / gas station / three hundred nineteen dollars**.
2. **locking the card / replacement / dispute / provisional credit**.
3. **five to seven business days / temporary credit**.

## Camera / UI tips
Money + process terms in one English turn is the money shot. End on temporary credit, then outro.
