# 09 — Banking (fraud / card lock)

**Setting:** Bank fraud specialist on a verified call.  
**Roles:** Bank agent (EN) · Customer (LEP)  
**Length:** ~20 seconds spoken  
**Why this reel:** Authorization, chargeback, temporary credit — real phone-banking language.

## Turn order

1. **Agent (EN):** I see two charges in Miami that you say you didn't authorize — one at a gas station and one online for three hundred nineteen dollars.
2. **Customer:** [LEP]
3. **Agent (EN):** I'm locking the card now and issuing a replacement. We'll open a dispute and post a provisional credit while we investigate.
4. **Customer:** [LEP]
5. **Agent (EN):** You'll get the new card in five to seven business days. Monitor your online banking for the temporary credit.
