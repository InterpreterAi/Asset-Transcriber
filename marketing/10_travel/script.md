# 10 — Travel (airline rebooking)

**Setting:** Airline rebooking desk after a missed connection.  
**Roles:** Agent (EN) · Passenger (LEP)  
**Length:** ~20 seconds spoken  
**Why this reel:** Flight numbers, standby, lodging voucher — travel ops language interpreters hear constantly.

## Turn order

1. **Agent (EN):** Your inbound flight arrived late, so you missed the connection to Chicago. I can put you on the next available, flight two-six-four, departing at seven-forty p.m.
2. **Passenger:** [LEP]
3. **Agent (EN):** That one's full coach, but I can waitlist you for standby, or confirm you tomorrow morning at six-ten with a hotel voucher tonight.
4. **Passenger:** [LEP]
5. **Agent (EN):** Confirmed for tomorrow, six-ten a.m. Here's your lodging voucher and meal coupons. Gate information will update in the app.
