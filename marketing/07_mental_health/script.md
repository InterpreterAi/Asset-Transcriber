# 07 — Mental health (intake)

**Setting:** Behavioral health clinician doing a brief safety / intake check.  
**Roles:** Clinician (EN) · Client (LEP)  
**Length:** ~22 seconds spoken  
**Why this reel:** Careful, real phrasing — SI screening language without sensationalism.

## Turn order

1. **Clinician (EN):** Thanks for coming in. Over the past two weeks, how often have you felt down, hopeless, or had little interest in things you usually enjoy?
2. **Client:** [LEP]
3. **Clinician (EN):** I also need to ask: have you had any thoughts of hurting yourself, or a plan to end your life?
4. **Client:** [LEP]
5. **Clinician (EN):** I'm glad you told me. We'll make a safety plan today, and I'm referring you to our crisis counselor before you leave.
