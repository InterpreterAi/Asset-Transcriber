# Recording notes — 07 Mental health

## Who speaks first
**Clinician (English)** — warm, unhurried.

## Pace & pauses
- Longer silence after the PHQ-style question (**~0.7s**) — clinically realistic.
- Safety question: clear, flat tone; no drama.
- Client answer should be quiet and natural, not theatrical.

## Overlaps
Absolutely none. Do not interrupt the client’s safety answer.

## When the demo should shine
1. **down / hopeless / little interest** — mood vocabulary.
2. **thoughts of hurting yourself / plan to end your life** — sensitive phrasing must translate carefully and completely.
3. **safety plan / crisis counselor** — next-step clinical language.

## Content caution
This is a demo of interpreter support, not a crisis resource. Keep takes respectful. Prefer this reel for professional interpreter audiences.

## Camera / UI tips
Softer lighting if filming faces; on product-only capture, hold the safety-plan line, then outro.
