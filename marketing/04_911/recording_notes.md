# Recording notes — 04 911

## Who speaks first
**Dispatcher (English):** “Nine-one-one, what is your emergency?”

## Pace & pauses
- Caller may sound stressed but still leave **~0.3s** gaps so STT can segment.
- Address must be crystal clear on both sides.
- Dispatcher confirmation of address is the hero line — do not rush it.

## Overlaps
Avoid true overlap. Mild urgency in voice is fine; talking over each other will muddy the reel.

## When the demo should shine
1. **chest pain / breathing** (LEP) → accurate symptom translation into English column.
2. **awake and breathing** — classic EMD question pair.
3. **fourteen-twenty West Maple, apartment three-B** — address echo shows the product catching critical detail.

## Camera / UI tips
Stacked or side-by-side both work; prefer side-by-side so address appears in both columns. Cut to outro right after ambulance dispatch line.
