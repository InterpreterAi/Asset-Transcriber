# 04 — 911 (emergency dispatch)

**Setting:** 911 call-taker with LEP caller reporting chest pain.  
**Roles:** Dispatcher (EN) · Caller (LEP)  
**Length:** ~18 seconds spoken (tight, urgent — still consecutive, not chaotic)  
**Why this reel:** High stakes, short questions, address + symptoms. Shows calm consecutive under pressure.

## Turn order

1. **Dispatcher (EN):** Nine-one-one, what is your emergency?
2. **Caller:** [LEP]
3. **Dispatcher (EN):** Okay. Is he awake and breathing? What is the address?
4. **Caller:** [LEP]
5. **Dispatcher (EN):** Stay on the line. I'm sending an ambulance to fourteen-twenty West Maple, apartment three-B.
