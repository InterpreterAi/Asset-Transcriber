# Recording notes — 03 Insurance

## Who speaks first
**Adjuster (English)** with claim number.

## Pace & pauses
- Claim number digit-by-digit or grouped: “four-seven-two-nine” (clear STT).
- After the liability/deductible explanation: full pause before LEP — that turn is dense.

## Overlaps
None. Adjuster finishes the coverage sentence completely.

## When the demo should shine
1. **claim number four-seven-two-nine** — digits stay intact.
2. **comprehensive deductible / liability / bumper repair** — insurance register.
3. **rental reimbursement / thirty dollars a day** — benefit + amount.

## Camera / UI tips
Keep LIVE timer visible. End on the rental line for a natural “problem solved” close.
