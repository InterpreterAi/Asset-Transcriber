# 03 — Insurance (auto claim)

**Setting:** Claims adjuster call after a fender-bender.  
**Roles:** Adjuster (EN) · Policyholder (LEP)  
**Length:** ~20 seconds spoken  
**Why this reel:** Deductible, liability, rental reimbursement — terms people hear on real claim calls.

## Turn order

1. **Adjuster (EN):** I'm calling about claim number four-seven-two-nine for the accident on Oak Street.
2. **Policyholder:** [LEP]
3. **Adjuster (EN):** Based on the police report, the other driver was found at fault. Your comprehensive deductible still applies to glass, but liability should cover the bumper repair.
4. **Policyholder:** [LEP]
5. **Adjuster (EN):** You also have rental reimbursement for up to thirty dollars a day while the car is in the shop.
