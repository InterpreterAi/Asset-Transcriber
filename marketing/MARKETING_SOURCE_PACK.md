## 5b. Security, privacy & aggressive questions (study this)

**Official trust line (memorize):**  
We describe practices carefully **without claiming certifications we have not earned**.  
HIPAA-focused thinking guides our design. Privacy-first. Interpreter-controlled sessions.

### Hard facts you CAN say (from Security page)

| Fact | Say this |
|------|----------|
| Audio storage | **We do not store or retain audio recordings of conversations.** Audio is handled in **real time** for temporary text output. |
| Call / transcript storage | **No call recordings, transcripts, or interpretation content are stored on our servers.** |
| How audio works | Processed as a **live stream** for captions/assist — **not** bulk-uploaded recordings. |
| Transport | Session streams use **encrypted transport**. Design favors **ephemeral** handling over bulk retention. |
| Data selling | **We do not sell user data.** |
| Control | **You** decide how/when the workspace runs. Tool **supports** interpretation — does **not** replace the interpreter. |
| Employer rules | Users must still follow **employer / platform confidentiality** policies. |
| HIPAA | **HIPAA-focused architecture / HIPAA-focused thinking** — **never** “HIPAA certified” unless leadership updates this pack. |

### Copy-paste replies (calm, not defensive)

**“Do you save my sessions / recordings?”**  
> No. InterpreterAI does not store or retain audio recordings. Audio is processed in real time for temporary captions/assist text. Call recordings, transcripts, and interpretation content are not stored on our servers. Details: app.interpreterai.org/security

**“Is this HIPAA compliant / certified?”**  
> The product is designed with HIPAA-focused thinking and privacy-conscious architecture. We describe practices carefully and do not claim certifications we have not earned. Full trust center: app.interpreterai.org/security — happy to walk your compliance contact through it.

**“Where does my audio go?”**  
> It’s a live stream for real-time captions and translation assistance—not a bulk upload you leave sitting on a server. Encrypted transport; ephemeral handling by design. → app.interpreterai.org/security

**“Can my agency / hospital use this?”**  
> Many teams review our security & privacy page before rollout. We’re built for regulated workflows with interpreter control and minimized retention patterns. Send them: app.interpreterai.org/security and app.interpreterai.org/privacy

**“Are you selling my data?”**  
> No. We do not sell user data. Personal information operates the service—not ad networks.

**“What if my company forbids tools?”**  
> You remain responsible for following employer and contracting-platform policies. InterpreterAI is assistive infrastructure—you stay in charge of professional judgment.

**“Prove it.”**  
> Don’t argue in comments. Send the link once:  
> https://app.interpreterai.org/security  
> Offer: “If your compliance person wants a walkthrough, we can do that.”

### Tone rules when people get aggressive

1. **Stay calm** — short answers, no sarcasm.  
2. **Facts + link** — never invent BAA / SOC2 / “certified” status.  
3. **One reply**, then invite DM / email — don’t fight in public threads.  
4. If asked for legal guarantees → **escalate to founder / support**, don’t freestyle.  
5. Always separate: **product privacy design** vs **their employer’s rules**.

### What NOT to say (even if pressured)

- “We’re 100% HIPAA compliant / certified.”  
- “Nothing ever touches a server.” (streaming still processes audio in real time — be precise)  
- “You can ignore your hospital’s policy.”  
- “We never keep any logs of any kind.” (don’t invent log policy)  
- Anything about BAAs, audits, or insurance unless leadership gives you approved language.

## 7. Reel structure (must match videos)

Every marketing reel should follow this spine (35s system):

| Time (approx) | Segment | What goes here |
|---------------|---------|----------------|
| 0–2s | **Brand intro** | Logo — locked |
| 2–5s | **Hook** | One bold line that stops the scroll |
| 5–14s | **Problem** | Pain the interpreter feels |
| 14–25s | **Solution** | Ideally **workspace demo** (live captions/turns) — or clear solution text until demo is cut in |
| 25–28s | **Result** | Short payoff / transformation |
| 28–35s | **Brand outro** | Logo · slogan · “Built for professional interpreters” · “62 languages” · **Start Free…** · **app.interpreterai.org** — locked |

**Locked = do not rewrite.** Same outro on every reel = brand recognition.
