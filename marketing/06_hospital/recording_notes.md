# Recording notes — 06 Hospital

## Who speaks first
**Triage nurse (English).**

## Pace & pauses
- Pain scale question: leave space for “eight” answer.
- Symptom checklist can be one breath; patient answers in one block.
- Explain **NPO** in English in the same sentence (“nothing by mouth”) so the reel teaches the acronym.

## Overlaps
None. ED energy without crosstalk.

## When the demo should shine
1. **zero to ten / abdominal pain** ↔ numeric pain score from LEP.
2. **vomiting, fever, lower right side** — appendicitis-flavored triage.
3. **NPO / IV / fluids and labs** — hospital shorthand that still translates clearly.

## Camera / UI tips
Dark theme looks clinical on camera. Hold the NPO/IV line before outro.
