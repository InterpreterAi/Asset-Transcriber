# 06 — Hospital (ED triage)

**Setting:** Emergency department triage nurse.  
**Roles:** Nurse (EN) · Patient (LEP)  
**Length:** ~22 seconds spoken  
**Why this reel:** Triage questions, pain scale, NPO / IV — hospital floor language.

## Turn order

1. **Nurse (EN):** I'm the triage nurse. On a scale of zero to ten, how bad is your abdominal pain right now?
2. **Patient:** [LEP]
3. **Nurse (EN):** Any vomiting, fever, or pain in the lower right side? When did you last eat or drink?
4. **Patient:** [LEP]
5. **Nurse (EN):** Okay. We're keeping you NPO — nothing by mouth — and I'm starting an IV for fluids and labs.
