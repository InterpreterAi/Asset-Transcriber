# Recording notes — 01 Medical

## Who speaks first
**Provider (English)** opens. Patient does not speak until the greeting is finished.

## Pace & pauses
- After each English turn: **0.4–0.6s** silence before LEP reply (room for consecutive interpretation on screen).
- Do **not** overlap provider and patient.
- Speak numbers clearly: “eight point two”, “one thousand milligrams”, “twice a day”.

## Overlaps
None. Clean consecutive style. If the patient starts early, cut and retake.

## When the demo should shine
1. **A1C / 8.2** — show accurate number capture in Original + Translation.
2. **metformin / one thousand milligrams twice a day** — dose + frequency in the same segment.
3. **hypoglycemia** — technical term lands correctly in the target language (not a vague “low sugar” paraphrase if the engine keeps the clinical word).

## Camera / UI tips
Start Record Mode intro → workspace already on EN↔ES (or EN↔AR / EN↔ZH) → begin audio on the first English line. Hold on the hypoglycemia line for one beat before outro.
