# 05 — Pharmacy (counseling)

**Setting:** Retail pharmacist counseling on a new antibiotic.  
**Roles:** Pharmacist (EN) · Patient (LEP)  
**Length:** ~20 seconds spoken  
**Why this reel:** Drug name, dosing schedule, interaction warning — dense but everyday.

## Turn order

1. **Pharmacist (EN):** This is amoxicillin five hundred milligrams. Take one capsule three times a day until it's gone — even if you feel better.
2. **Patient:** [LEP]
3. **Pharmacist (EN):** Are you allergic to penicillin? And please don't take it with antacids within two hours.
4. **Patient:** [LEP]
5. **Pharmacist (EN):** If you get a rash, swelling, or trouble breathing, stop the medication and go to the ER.
