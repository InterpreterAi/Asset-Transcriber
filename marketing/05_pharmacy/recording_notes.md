# Recording notes — 05 Pharmacy

## Who speaks first
**Pharmacist (English)** with drug + dose.

## Pace & pauses
- Enunciate **amoxicillin** and **penicillin** — easy to mush together.
- Pause after the first counseling block before patient confirms.
- ER warning line: slightly slower; it’s the safety punch.

## Overlaps
None. Patient questions come only in their turns.

## When the demo should shine
1. **amoxicillin five hundred milligrams** + **three times a day**.
2. **penicillin** allergy check.
3. **antacids within two hours** — timing constraint.
4. **rash / swelling / trouble breathing / ER** — red-flag list.

## Camera / UI tips
Glossary optional off for this cut — let raw terms show. End on ER warning, then outro.
