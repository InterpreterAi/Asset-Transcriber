# 02 — Legal (attorney–client consult)

**Setting:** Remote attorney consult before a hearing.  
**Roles:** Attorney (EN) · Client (LEP)  
**Length:** ~22 seconds spoken  
**Why this reel:** Privilege-adjacent tone, court dates, and “plea / continuance” vocabulary without sounding scripted.

## Turn order

1. **Attorney (EN):** Before we go further, this call is confidential under attorney-client privilege.
2. **Client:** [LEP]
3. **Attorney (EN):** The prosecutor offered a plea to a lesser charge. If we decline, I can ask the court for a continuance to review discovery.
4. **Client:** [LEP]
5. **Attorney (EN):** Your next court date is March fourteenth at nine a.m. Do not miss that appearance.
