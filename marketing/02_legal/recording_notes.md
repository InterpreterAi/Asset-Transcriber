# Recording notes — 02 Legal

## Who speaks first
**Attorney (English)** opens with the privilege line. Client waits.

## Pace & pauses
- Privilege line: steady, not rushed — it’s the trust beat of the reel.
- After “discovery”: **~0.5s** pause before client answers.
- Date/time spoken slowly: “March fourteenth” / “nine a.m.”

## Overlaps
None. Client never talks over counsel.

## When the demo should shine
1. **attorney-client privilege** — full phrase in Original; accurate legal sense in Translation.
2. **plea / lesser charge / continuance / discovery** — cluster of legal terms in one English turn.
3. **March fourteenth at nine a.m.** — date and time survive cleanly.

## Camera / UI tips
Side-by-side layout. Hold the court-date segment on screen for the end card beat before outro.
