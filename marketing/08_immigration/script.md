# 08 — Immigration (asylum prep)

**Setting:** Accredited representative / attorney preparing a client for an asylum interview.  
**Roles:** Representative (EN) · Applicant (LEP)  
**Length:** ~22 seconds spoken  
**Why this reel:** Credible fear, persecution, country of origin — immigration hearing register.

## Turn order

1. **Representative (EN):** In your asylum interview, the officer will ask why you fear returning to your country of origin.
2. **Applicant:** [LEP]
3. **Representative (EN):** You need to describe specific harm or threats — persecution based on your political opinion, not just general crime.
4. **Applicant:** [LEP]
5. **Representative (EN):** Tell the truth. If you don't remember a date, say you don't remember. Inconsistencies can hurt credibility.
