# Recording notes — 08 Immigration

## Who speaks first
**Representative (English)** sets the interview frame.

## Pace & pauses
- “country of origin” / “persecution” / “political opinion” — deliberate enunciation.
- After the persecution explanation, give the applicant a full beat to answer with the January incident.
- Final credibility instruction: calm authority.

## Overlaps
None. Applicant’s narrative turn should be uninterrupted.

## When the demo should shine
1. **asylum interview / country of origin**.
2. **persecution / political opinion** vs **general crime** — precise legal distinction.
3. **inconsistencies / credibility** — hearing vocabulary.

## Camera / UI tips
Best as EN↔ES first cut. Hold the credibility line for the close, then outro.
